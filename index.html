About us

Corporate catering simplified: fresh, honest and on time

Home-cooked food for your team, without the shortcuts.

At Salt & Pepper, we believe good food brings a team together. We supply snacks, breakfast, tiffin and live counters to offices, apartments and events around Chennai.

We are not a factory kitchen. We are a small group of neighbours cooking in our own home in Thiruvanmiyur: fresh vegetables, new oil every day, branded spices, no MSG and no reheating.

We serve your team the same food we serve our own kids and parents.
