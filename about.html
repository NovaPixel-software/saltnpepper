Who we are

Cooked at home, served with care

Salt & Pepper Food Chain is a home-kitchen food business in Chennai. We are a small group of neighbours who cook together. We do not hire labourers and we do not run a factory kitchen.

Our idea is simple: serve our customers the same food we serve our own kids and parents. Fresh ingredients, clean oil, real spices and no shortcuts.
